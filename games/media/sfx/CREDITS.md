# Sound credits

Both sounds are edited from Wikimedia Commons recordings that are public domain / CC0.

- `applause.wav`: "Clapping hurray" by starlite, public domain.
  Trimmed to 10.5 s with a fade-out, normalized.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clapping_hurray.ogg
- `airhorn.wav`: "Leslie A200-156" (single-trumpet locomotive air horn) by HarveyHenkelmann, CC0.
  Sped up 3× (≈153 Hz → ≈460 Hz, roughly a handheld canister horn), lightly saturated,
  and cut into the meme rhythm: two short blasts, one long.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leslie_A200-156.ogg
